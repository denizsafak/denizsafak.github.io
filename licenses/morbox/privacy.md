**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deniz Şafak ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of Morbox, handles information when you use the Morbox mobile app ("App"). A Turkish translation is available at [Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)](https://denizsafak.github.io/licenses/morbox/privacy-tr/). Where the two versions conflict, the Turkish version prevails for users in Türkiye and the English version prevails for everyone else.

> **In short**
> * Morbox works on your device. It has no accounts, and we operate no servers that receive your files.
> * Crash reports and usage statistics are optional and off by default. You choose each one separately, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
> * If you allow them, they are sent to Google (Firebase) and linked to a random installation identifier, not to your name or an account.
> * Some features use Google services (maps, text recognition, background removal, and document scanning) and Apple's document scanner; Section 4 lists them in a table.

## 1. Who is responsible
The data controller is Deniz Şafak, Türkiye. You can contact us at **denizsafak98@gmail.com**.

## 2. What stays on your device
Files you select, convert, scan, or create are processed on your device. We operate no servers that receive your files, and the App has no user accounts. Your settings, preferences, game scores, and the last map view in the map tool are stored on your device. You can delete this local data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. "Reset all settings" in Settings also erases it, including the stored map view.

## 3. Optional crash reports and usage statistics
The App can send crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to Google (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), which provides these services and processes the data on our behalf.

**Your choice.** When you first open the App, a consent screen at the end of the onboarding tour lets you allow both, choose only one, or decline both. Nothing is sent by these two features until you allow it, and the App works the same if you decline. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Telemetry, and the App stops sending the data you turn off. If you allow them, the data is collected automatically by the App's Firebase software.

**What is collected, if you allow it.**
* Crash reports: the type of error, technical details about where it happened, and information about the app and device, such as the app version, operating system version, device model, available memory and storage, and the state of the app when it crashed.
* Usage statistics: events such as opening the App, which tool you open, and whether a conversion started, finished, or failed (with a general duration range and an error code), together with the events Google Analytics collects automatically (such as first open, session start, and screen views), the app version, language, device model, and operating system.
* Google also receives your IP address when this data is sent. Google Analytics uses it to derive an approximate location (country, region, and city). The App does not read or send your GPS location for these features.
* This data is linked to random installation identifiers created by the Firebase software, not to your name, your email address, or an account. Under some laws, these identifiers are still personal data.

The App is designed to redact and not include file names, file paths, file contents, or text you enter in these reports. However, crash reports and error messages generated by the operating system or by third-party libraries may occasionally include such details or technical strings by their nature, which we do not control.

**Why.** To find and fix crashes and errors, and to understand which features are used and how reliably they work. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

**Legal basis.** Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR; explicit consent under Article 5 of the KVKK). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

**How long it is kept.** Our Google Analytics configuration keeps event data for 2 months. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days, as described in [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). After that, Google deletes them.

**Transfers abroad.** If you allow these features, the data is sent to Google and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under legally recognized transfer safeguards such as standard data protection clauses (including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK extension).

**More from Google.** [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 5. Other things to know
* **Permissions.** The App asks for permissions only when a feature needs them, for example camera access (scanner, mirror, color picker, document scanning), access to files and media you choose, notifications (timers and long conversions), and location (only the "My location" button, and to join a Wi-Fi network from a scanned QR code). You can change permissions in your device settings.
* **QR codes and links.** When you tap a link, phone number, email, or location from a scanned code, the App opens another app, which handles it under its own policy. Joining a Wi-Fi network from a QR code passes the credentials in the code to your device's operating system.
* **This policy in the App.** The Privacy Policy and Terms screens in the App load their text from GitHub, which receives standard connection information such as your IP address.
* **Security.** Your files are processed on your device and are never sent to us. Data sent to Google for the optional features and the map is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We keep no server-side copy of your personal data.

## 7. Children
Morbox is for people aged 13 or older, or the higher minimum age in your country. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Allowing crash reports or usage statistics is meant for people who are old enough to consent in their country; if you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. These rights come from laws such as the GDPR in the EU and UK, the KVKK in Türkiye, the CCPA/CPRA in California, other US state privacy laws, and similar laws in other countries (for example Brazil's LGPD or Canada's PIPEDA). In Türkiye, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board; under Article 11 of the KVKK you can also learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, and claim compensation for unlawful processing. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we honor valid privacy requests from users wherever they live.

To make a request, email **denizsafak98@gmail.com** with the subject "Privacy Request". We respond within the time required by law — for example, within one month under the GDPR and within 30 days under the KVKK.

**What this means in practice.** The App has no user accounts, and we operate no servers that receive or store your personal data. The optional crash reports and usage statistics are sent directly from the App to Google and are linked only to random installation identifiers, not to your name or email address. We can see usage and crash information in Google's tools, but we cannot identify you or single out your records from it. This is the situation described in Article 11 of the GDPR (processing that does not require identification): we cannot look up, export, or delete the data of a specific person on our side, and we do not collect additional information just to identify you. Instead, you can stop collection at any time in Settings → Privacy & Telemetry, and previously sent data is deleted automatically after the periods in Section 3. For data that Google processes for its own purposes (for example, the map service or Google Play services), you can find Google's privacy information linked above.
